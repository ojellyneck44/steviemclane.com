I'm Stevie McLane, a UX designer and creative director in Denton, Texas.

About

I'm working toward a BFA in UX Design at the University of North Texas, class of 2029. The problems I care about most belong to the people a standard product flow quietly leaves out: a patient whose anxiety peaks in the hour before an appointment, or an artist whose releases change style every few weeks and still need to read as one body of work.

I start with research and paper. Interviews, personas and journey maps show me where things break, sketches let me test answers quickly, and Figma is where the decisions get finished. I also write code, so I can carry a design through to a working product and test it on real devices.
